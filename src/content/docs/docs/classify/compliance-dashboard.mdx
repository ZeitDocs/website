title: Compliance Dashboard
description: See how classification is used across every space of your site, and export it as CSV.

The **Compliance** tab gives Confluence admins one view of classification across
the whole site: which spaces use it, how many pages carry a classification level,
and which spaces need a look.

The [Classification Report](/docs/classify/classification-report/) answers the same
question for a single space, page by page. The Compliance tab answers it for
every space at once, with counts.

## Who can use it

Only **Confluence admins**. The tab is part of **Classification Templates**
(Apps → *Classification Templates*), and the app checks the Confluence
Administrator permission again before it returns any data.

## Opening the dashboard

1. Open **Classification Templates**.
2. Select the **Compliance** tab.

The app scans every space of the site. While it runs, the tab shows
*Scanning N of M spaces…* and the figures fill in as each group of spaces is
counted. When it finishes, the top right shows **Scanned at** and the time.
Select **Refresh** to scan again.

On a large site the first scan can take a little while. If you switch to the
**Templates** tab and back, the dashboard keeps its results and does not scan
again.

## Choosing which spaces to include

The scope control at the top applies to everything on the tab: the figures, the
charts and the table.

| Scope | Includes |
|---|---|
| **Site spaces** (default) | Team and project spaces |
| **Personal spaces** | Users' personal spaces |
| **All spaces** | Both |

Personal spaces are left out by default because a site usually has many of
them, and most never use classification. Including them would make the
coverage figure look lower than it really is for team content.

If the site has archived spaces, an **Include archived spaces** checkbox appears.
Archived spaces are left out unless you select it.

## Space status

Every space has one status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Enabled** | Classification is on and the space has levels. Pages can be classified. |
| **No levels** | Classification is on, but the space has no levels, so nobody can classify a page. |
| **Not enabled** | Classification is switched off, or was never set up in the space. |
| **Blocked** | [Space Access Control](/docs/classify/space-access-control/) does not allow classification in this space. |

**Blocked** wins over every other status: a blocked space is shown as Blocked even
if its classification was switched on earlier.

## The overview figures

### Spaces with classification enabled

The large percentage is the share of spaces in scope that are **Enabled**. The
bar below it splits the same spaces by status. Hover over a part of the bar to
see its count.

Coverage is measured in **spaces**, not pages. Classify does not count the total
number of pages in a space, so it cannot show a "percentage of pages classified".

### Stat tiles

| Tile | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Classified pages** | Pages that carry a classification level in the spaces in scope, and how many of them were classified in the last 12 weeks. |
| **Enabled, nothing classified** | Enabled spaces where no page has been classified yet. |
| **Pages on removed levels** | Classified pages whose level was later deleted from the space, or removed by a template update. |
| **Classified pages left behind** | Classified pages in spaces where classification is now switched off or blocked. |

When a tile reports a problem, select **Show these spaces** to filter the table
to the spaces concerned.

:::note[Why pages on removed levels and left-behind pages matter]
Classify does not remove anything from a page on its own. A page on a removed
level, or in a space where classification was switched off or blocked, **keeps
its label and its view and edit restrictions**. Re-classify these pages, or
remove the label and restrictions in Confluence, so that what readers see
matches your current policy.
:::

## Charts

### Classification activity

Pages by the week they were **last** classified, in enabled spaces, over the last
12 weeks. Weeks start on Monday (UTC). The current week is drawn lighter because
it is still in progress. Hover over a week to see its count.

Re-classifying a page moves it to the week of its latest change, so the chart
shows recent activity, not a complete history.

### Levels in use

Classified pages per level across enabled spaces. Each space has its own levels,
so levels are grouped **by name**: an *Internal* level in two spaces is counted
as one row. The coloured dot is the level's colour.

## The Spaces table

One row per space in scope, with its status, template, number of classified
pages, a bar of pages per level, and the date of the last classification.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/screenshots/classify/17-compliance-spaces-table.png" alt="The Spaces table with one space expanded to show its levels and page counts, and another space marked Not enabled with classified pages left behind." />
  <figcaption>The Spaces table, with one space expanded.</figcaption>
</figure>

- **Search** by space name, space key or template name.
- **Filter by status**: All, Enabled, Not enabled, Blocked, or **Needs attention**.
- **Filter by template** to see the spaces that use one global template, or those with no template.
- **Sort** by Space, Status, Template, Classified pages or Last classified: click the column header.
- **Select a row** to expand it. You see each level with its page count and share,
  the space key, the template, the date of the last classification, and a link to
  open the space.

**Needs attention** lists the spaces with at least one of these problems: no
levels, pages on removed levels, classified pages left behind, nothing classified
yet, or a space that could not be scanned.

Pages on a level the space no longer has are drawn **hatched** in the
*Pages per level* bar, and marked **Removed level** when you expand the row.

A very large space shows its page count with a **+** (for example `5,000+`).
This means the count stopped at that number and the space has at least that
many classified pages.

If a space could not be scanned, it shows **Not scanned**. Select **Refresh** to
try again.

## Exporting to CSV

Select **Export CSV** above the table to download the table as a CSV file. The
button is available once every space has been scanned.

The file has one row per space, **as filtered**: the scope, search and filters
you have applied decide which rows are exported. Columns:

| Column | Content |
|---|---|
| Space, Space key, Space type | Name, key, and *Site* or *Personal* (with *(archived)* when it applies) |
| Classification status | Enabled, No levels, Not enabled or Blocked |
| Template | The global template the space uses, if any |
| Classified pages | Number of classified pages |
| Count is a lower bound | `yes` when the count stopped at its limit (shown as `+` on screen) |
| Pages on removed levels | Number of pages on levels the space no longer has |
| Last classified (UTC) | Date and time of the last classification |
| Pages per level | For example `Public: 12; Internal: 30`. Removed levels are marked *(removed)*. |

The file is created in your browser. Nothing is sent to an external service.

## What the dashboard does not show

The dashboard shows **counts only**. It does not list page titles, and it never
shows the groups or labels attached to levels. To see the pages of one space,
open its [Classification Report](/docs/classify/classification-report/).
