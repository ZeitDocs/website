Space Settings is where a space administrator turns classification **on** for a
space, chooses the **classification levels** people can apply, and sets the
banner **size**. You reach it from your space's **Classification** settings page,
or through the byline onboarding flow.

The page has three sections, top to bottom:

- [Status](#status) — turn classification on or off for the space.
- [Classification levels](#classification-levels) — the levels people can apply, and how each banner looks.
- [Header image size](#header-image-size) — the banner height for the whole space.

## Status

A card showing whether classification is **Enabled** or **Disabled** for the space.

- When **disabled**, the only action is the primary **Enable classification** button. Until you enable it, page editors cannot classify pages.
- When **enabled**, the levels and other controls appear below.
- When your levels came from a **global template**, the card also names that template and warns that your levels are template-managed (see below).

## Classification levels

This is the heart of the space configuration: the list of classification levels
page editors can pick from.

### Where classification levels come from

You have two ways to populate levels:

1. **Apply a global template** (recommended) — use **Add from template** to copy a ready-made set of levels published by your Confluence admin. This keeps your space consistent with the rest of the organisation. Once applied, the levels are **copied into your space**.
2. **Build your own** — use **Add level** to create levels from scratch.

The status card names the **template** your levels came from, so you always know
the source.

:::caution[Template-managed levels]
While your space is linked to a global template, the template is the source of
truth for its levels:

> *"Levels here are managed by the **&lt;template&gt;** template. If an
> administrator re-applies it to this space, changes you make here are replaced.
> Unlink to manage levels independently — this clears the levels currently in the
> space."*

You can still edit levels locally. Editing the template itself changes nothing in
your space — the replacement happens when a Confluence administrator
**re-applies** the template to it.

If your space needs its own levels permanently, ask your Confluence admin to
adjust the template, or use
[Unlink](#unlinking-from-a-global-template) and build the levels yourself. Note
that unlinking **deletes the levels currently in the space** — it does not keep a
local copy of them.
:::

### What is in a classification level

Each classification level has:

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Name** | For example *Public*, *Internal*, *Confidential*. Shown in the byline and banner. |
| **Colour** | The colour dot in the byline and the colour-band banner. |
| **Description** | Short explanation; shown on hover and on image banners. |
| **View restriction groups** | Confluence groups allowed to **view** pages at this level. |
| **Edit restriction groups** | Confluence groups allowed to **edit** pages at this level. |
| **Label** | The Confluence label added to classified pages (for example `INTERNAL`). |
| **Page banner** | Whether the level shows a visual banner, and how it looks. Designed in the [Banner Studio](/docs/classify/banner-studio/) — see [Page banner](#page-banner). |

:::caution[These groups control page access]
The view and edit groups directly change who can access a page. Double-check
them before letting page editors classify important pages — applying a level
restricts the page to exactly these groups.
:::

### Reordering levels

Once you have levels, you can **drag and drop** to reorder them. The order is the
order page editors see in the picker.

### Page banner

The **Page Banner** section of the level form shows a preview of the level's
visual banner and a one-line summary of its design.

- **None** — no banner for this level. The byline dot still shows the level.
- **Show banner** — the level has a banner.
- **Open banner designer** — opens the [Banner Studio](/docs/classify/banner-studio/), where you choose the background (colour, gradient, preset image or your own upload), the text position, size and colour, and check readability.
- **Remove** — removes the banner from the level.

After you click **Apply** in the studio, the preview shows your design. Click
**Save Changes** to save the level. Cancelling the level form discards the banner
changes too.

Each level's banner is designed on its own.

### Manage global templates

If you are also a **Confluence admin**, a **"Manage global templates ↗"** link
appears in the levels section header, taking you to the global template library.
It is hidden for non-admins and while you are editing a level.

## Header image size

A secondary setting at the bottom controls the banner **height** for the whole
space. It applies to every image and colour banner in the space.

| Size | Height |
|---|---|
| **Small** | 80px |
| **Medium** *(the default)* | 160px |
| **Large** | 240px |
| **Auto** | Grows to fit the description, between 80px and 320px |

**Auto** is the option to choose when your level descriptions run long. The fixed
sizes shrink the text until it fits the banner; **Auto** keeps the text at its
configured size and grows the banner instead.

You can also choose the size from the size cards in the
[Banner Studio](/docs/classify/banner-studio/#choosing-the-banner-size), where
you can compare all four first. See
[Page Banner](/docs/classify/page-banner/#banner-size) for the full comparison.

:::note[When a global template sets the size]
If your Confluence admin has set a header image size on the **global template**
your levels came from, a note here tells you which size that is.

Your local choice still applies day to day, but re-applying the template will
replace it — in the same way it replaces the levels.
:::

## Unlinking from a global template

If your levels came from a global template and you want to start fresh:

<Steps>

1. Use the **Unlink** action.
2. Confirm in the warning dialog.
3. The space resets to **disabled with no levels**, and is removed from the template's linked-spaces list.

</Steps>

After unlinking you can re-apply a different global template or build levels
manually.

## Blocked spaces

If your Confluence admin has **blocked** this space via Space Access Control, the
whole settings page is replaced with an informational message and all controls
are hidden — you cannot enable classification here. See
[Space Access Control](/docs/classify/space-access-control/).
