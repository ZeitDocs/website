The **Banner Studio** is a full-screen designer for the visual banner of a
classification level. You choose the background, place and size the text, and
check that the result is readable, with a live preview of the banner as page
readers will see it.

The studio is available wherever classification levels are edited:

- **Space Settings**, for a space's own levels. You need to be a space admin.
- The **global template editor**, for the levels of a global template. You need to be a Confluence admin.

## Opening the studio

<Steps>

1. Open a classification level for editing in [Space Settings](/docs/classify/space-settings/#page-banner) or in a [global template](/docs/classify/global-templates/#creating-or-editing-a-template).
2. In the **Page Banner** section, choose **Show banner**.
3. Click **Open banner designer**.
4. Design the banner, then click **Apply**.
5. Back in the level form, click **Save Changes**.

</Steps>

:::caution[Apply, then save the level]
**Apply** returns your design to the level form. The level itself is only saved
when you click **Save Changes**. If you cancel the level form, the banner changes
are discarded too.
:::

**Cancel** closes the studio without changing anything.

## The level form card

The **Page Banner** section of the level form shows:

- A **preview** of the banner.
- A one-line **summary**, for example *Gradient · Text on the left · Contrast 7.1:1*.
- **None** or **Show banner**. **None** turns the banner off for this level. The byline dot still shows the level.
- **Open banner designer** and **Remove**.

## Background

The left panel sets what is behind the text. Choose one of four sources:

| Source | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Colour** | A solid colour band. Use the level colour or pick a custom colour. |
| **Gradient** | A two-colour gradient with a start colour, an end colour and an angle. |
| **Preset** | One of the built-in background images. No upload needed. |
| **Upload** | Your own image, in JPEG or PNG format. |

**Tint by level colour** is available for Colour and Gradient. When it is on, the
colours come from the level's own colour, so every level that uses this style
shows its own colour.

### Uploaded images

For an uploaded image you can also set:

- **Focal point** — click the part of the image that must stay in view. The banner is wider and shorter than most photos, so the image is cropped to fit. The focal point decides which part is kept.
- **Zoom** — from 100% to 250%.

The image is stored when you click **Apply**. A very large or detailed image is
compressed. If it is still too large after compression, the studio asks you for a
smaller or simpler image.

**Replace** chooses a different image. **Remove** clears it.

## Text

The right-hand panels control the level name and description on the banner.

### Text position

Choose one of nine positions: top, middle or bottom, each on the left, in the
centre or on the right. Until you choose one, the text sits on the left.

### Text size and colour

| Setting | Range | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Level name** | 16–72 | 42 |
| **Description** | 10–32 | 20 |

These are design sizes, not final pixel sizes. Under each slider the studio shows
the size the text really has on the space's current
[banner size](/docs/classify/page-banner/#banner-size). A **Small** banner has
little room, so a large value may have no visible effect there. The studio tells
you when this happens.

You can also choose **Light text** or **Dark text**, and turn on a **Text shadow**.

### Legibility overlay

Image backgrounds only. A semi-transparent layer between the image and the text
makes the text easier to read:

- **Flat** — an even layer over the whole banner.
- **Left fade** — strongest behind the text on the left, fading out to the right.
- **Bottom fade** — strongest at the bottom, fading out upwards.

**Opacity** sets the strength, from 0% to 80%. With dark text, the overlay is
light instead of dark.

### Contrast

The **Contrast** panel estimates how readable the text is against its background.
It shows the contrast ratio and whether it passes WCAG AA for small text (4.5:1). WCAG is the web accessibility standard that most organisations
follow.

If the other text colour reads better, a button such as **Use dark text (8.2:1)**
switches to it in one click.

The check is a guide only. It never stops you from applying a design.

## The preview

The centre of the studio shows the banner on a sample Confluence page, using the
same component that draws the banner for page readers.

- **Wide page** and **Narrow page** show the banner at two page widths. The text fits differently on a narrow page.
- A status shows how the text fits: **Everything fits**, **Text reduced to fit**, **Text held at minimum size**, or **Description cut off**.
- **Preview at every size** shows the banner at **Small**, **Medium**, **Large** and **Auto** side by side, with the text size each one produces.

### Choosing the banner size

Click a size card to preview the banner at that size. What happens on **Apply**
depends on where you opened the studio:

| Opened from | Clicking a size card |
|---|---|
| **Space Settings** | Sets the banner size for the whole space. It applies to every level. |
| **Space Settings**, when the global template sets the size | Preview only. The template decides the size. To override it, use [Header image size](/docs/classify/space-settings/#header-image-size) in Space Settings. |
| **Global template editor** | Sets the template's banner size. **Not set** lets each space keep its own size. |

## Undo, redo and reset

- **Undo** and **Redo** step through your changes. You can also use **Ctrl+Z** and **Shift+Ctrl+Z**.
- **Reset to defaults** restores the default text and legibility settings. The background is kept.

Nothing is saved until you click **Apply**, so you can experiment freely.

## Banners created before the Banner Studio

Existing banners are not changed by the Banner Studio. A level keeps exactly the
banner it had until an admin changes it and saves the level.

When you open such a level, the studio shows a note: *"Created with the previous
editor. It opens exactly as page viewers see it, and nothing about it changes
unless you change it here."*

Two older cases have their own behaviour:

- **Text inside the image.** In the first version of Classify, the level name and description were painted into the header image. The studio shows *"Text is part of the image"* and holds back the text, position, overlay and contrast controls, because that text cannot be changed. Choose a new background to convert the level to live text.
- **A custom dragged position.** A text position dragged in the previous editor is kept and shown as **Custom (previous editor)**. It stays until you choose one of the nine positions.
